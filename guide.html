PROJECT GUIDE

How emergency dispatch works

This demonstration looks up the caller's location, finds nearby hospitals, compares demo ambulances by road travel time, and shares the routes with the driver dashboard.

01

Report the emergency

The Patient / User profile captures the caller's description and a supported location or landmark.

02

Understand the report

The browser-based AI classifies the emergency and extracts details. If the model is unavailable, local rule-based extraction is used.

03

Select and notify an ambulance

The dispatcher compares available ambulances by their shortest route to the emergency, then sends the request to the Ambulance profile.

04

Track progress and destination

The driver can accept the request, travel to the emergency, continue to the selected hospital, and report status updates to the caller dashboard.

OPENSTREETMAP ROUTING

How routes are found

OpenStreetMap finds the address and nearby hospitals. OSRM calculates driving routes and estimated times over the mapped road network.

Address and hospitals

OpenStreetMap searches the entered address and hospitals within 30 km of the incident.

Ambulance selection

For this demo, simulated ambulance locations are placed near the incident. OSRM compares their road travel times and selects the closest route. These are not real ambulance locations.

What you see

Both dashboards show the actual mapped road geometries and estimated drive distances. The animated vehicle position remains a simulation.

Prototype note: Ambulance locations and movement are simulated near the incident, not live vehicles or GPS. Routing and hospital data may be limited by public-service availability; drive estimates are not live traffic or emergency-vehicle navigation.
